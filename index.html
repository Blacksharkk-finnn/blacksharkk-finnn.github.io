*Case file 01 / Credit riskModel + credit memo

Fund the $350K draw?

A borrower with a $600,000 revolver balance asks to draw another $350,000 against $2.395M of receivables from 7 debtors. Every dollar removed from the base traces back to the invoice and the rule that removed it. Three scenarios, a 4 × 4 sensitivity grid, and unit tests against hand-calculated cases.

85% advance rate90-day cutoff50% cross-age25% concentration3% dilution reserveAffiliate, disputed excluded
  1. Aging matters far more than dilution. Going from 3% to 10% dilution costs about $90K of availability; a 15-day slowdown alone costs about $300K.
  2. One debtor is the cliff. $200K of its invoices are 82 and 87 days old, so 15 more days pushes them past the cutoff and the cross-age rule removes its current invoice too.
  3. One debtor is 42% of gross receivables. The concentration cap already removes about $506K in the base case, the biggest structural risk in the pool.
Credit memo recommendation Approve $300,000 of the $350,000 requested, which stays covered under moderate stress. Release the remaining $50,000 once the next borrowing-base certificate shows the at-risk invoices collected.
