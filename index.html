A revolver draw that didn't survive the stress test

Receivables borrowing-base stress test, credit risk. The question was whether a proposed revolver draw stays supported when receivables deteriorate.

I modelled a $1.6M receivables borrowing base in Google Sheets across 8 invoices and 6 debtors, applying the eligibility rules a lender would use, then stressed it with 30 extra days of aging and 6% dilution.
